05 — About

A creative collective.

Luminescent started with a simple goal: take on exciting challenges and build something amazing. Today we’re a collective of designers, programmers and techs who bring that to other people’s productions, from tours and festivals to galas and trade show floors across North America.

Lighting isn’t just about lighting a space. It’s about changing it. We keep things laid-back, professional and on point from the first call to the last cue.
